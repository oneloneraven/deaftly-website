# Ethics and sustainability

We try to build Deaftly ethically and sustainably, but that's hard to do completely in the tech world. This page aims to provide transparency about the tech used to build this app, and the impact of it, so that you can make a conscious choice about using it. Many of the big tech companies' tools and services are ubiquitous, so even if using them directly can be avoided, they will be used in secondary or tertiary systems. It's also difficult to claim true sustainability in tech, given the demand on power and cooling. There is even a significant social impact of major tech hubs where the cost of buying or renting property soars as tech talent moves to where the jobs are, pricing locals out of the market and directly increasing homelessness and rough sleeping.

## Accessibility

This site and the app are built to WCAG 2.2 AA. We test every page automatically for accessibility problems, in light and dark themes and at phone sizes, and we design for large text, high contrast and clear, plain English.

## No tracking

There's no tracking, advertising or analytics on this site or in the app, and no cookies on this site. We don't sell or share data, and we don't build profiles. Our [privacy page](/privacy/) has the details.

## Building with AI

We build Deaftly with the help of AI tools. It wouldn't exist otherwise. AI uses real energy and water, both to train the models and to run them.

We made a trade-off. We accepted that impact so we could build something useful for deaf people. We think the footprint of a small app like this is modest, but it isn't zero, and we'd rather say so.

## Who we buy from

We support the [Boycott, Divestment and Sanctions (BDS) movement](https://bdsmovement.net). We've checked every package, tool, framework and provider we use against BDS's consumer boycott targets and its No Tech for Oppression, Apartheid or Genocide campaign, and we check anything new before we adopt it.

- **Our source control platform is owned by a company on both lists.** It's the industry standard, so we use it to store our code. We'll stay on its free tier for as long as we can, so we aren't contributing financially to an organisation on the BDS list.

## We believe in the democratisation of tech

Deaftly is free for individual users. We don't put features behind paywalls, offer paid add-ons, or monetise through advertising or data harvesting. Everybody gets the same app. Why? Being deaf already brings extra costs and barriers, and a tool for being understood shouldn't add another one.

## The code is public

Deaftly's [code is public](https://github.com/oneloneraven/deaftly), so anyone can see how it works and what it does with your data. Every release is built in public and signed with the same key, so you can check that the app you install is the real one. See [how to check it](/download/#how).

Thanks for reading, and thanks for using Deaftly.
