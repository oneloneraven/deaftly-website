# Shops and counters

Communicating in shops when you're deaf is usually a quick question that turns into a guessing game. Deaftly keeps it short.

## Asking for something

The **Ask for help** template asks someone to help you find what you're looking for. Pick the till or a member of staff, or type something else.

## At the till

The **Paying** template asks whether you can pay by card and to see the total:

> I'm deaf. Can I pay by **card**? Please show me the total.

## Explaining you're deaf

When someone starts talking and you've missed it, the **Explain I'm deaf** template tells them, and asks them to type on your phone instead.

## Keeping hold of your phone

With **Face to face**, your words face the other person as you type and you never have to hand your phone over. That's useful at a busy counter.

## Tips

- Show any message full screen and switch to yellow on black or white on black in bright shop lighting.
- Make your own templates for the shops you use every week.
