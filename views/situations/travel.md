# Travel

Announcements, ticket desks and help points are built around hearing. When something changes at the last minute, you need an answer fast. Deaftly helps you ask and keep up with the answer.

## Buying a ticket

The **Buy a ticket** template asks for a ticket to wherever you're going. Type the station, then show it:

> Hi! I'm deaf. I'd like a ticket to **Leeds**, please. Please **type on my phone**.

## Platforms, delays and changes

Use **Type & show** to ask a member of staff which platform, or whether your train is delayed. Show it full screen so they can read it straight away.

If the answer is more than a word or two, switch to **Side by side** and let them type it. Their reply stays on the screen, so you can check it again on the way to the platform.

## Asking for help

The **Ask for help** template asks someone to help you find something. Pick the lift, the exit or a member of staff, or type something else, like the taxi rank.

## Tips

- Many UK train operators now offer BSL interpreting and staff with deaf awareness training. Check your operator's accessibility page before you travel.
- Make templates for journeys you do often.
