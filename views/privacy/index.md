# Privacy

This page explains what the Deaftly app keeps, what this website collects, why, and for how long.

## The app

Deaftly keeps what you put into it on your device. We, the people who make Deaftly, don't receive any of it, unless you send us a message from the app (see [Feedback](#feedback)).

<div class="table-scroll" role="region" aria-label="What the app keeps" tabindex="0">

| What | Why | How long |
|---|---|---|
| Your templates, and what you type into them. This can include names, health details or routines. | So you can use them again | Until you delete them or the app |
| Your first, last and legal name, if you add them | To fill in your name on templates and, if you turn it on, in chats | Until you change them or delete the app |
| Your colour and other settings | To set up chats and Show the way you like | Until you change them or delete the app |
| Chat messages | To hold the conversation | Only while the chat is open. They're deleted when you end the chat, unless you keep it. |
| Chats you choose to keep | So you can read them or carry on later | Until you delete them or the app |
| Camera pictures and the text read from them | To add text to a message | Pictures are never stored. Text is kept only if you add it to a template. |
| What the microphone hears during voice typing, and the text made from it | So the other person in a side by side chat can speak their replies | Sound is never stored. The text becomes a chat message, so it's kept only if you keep the chat. |

</div>

### Android backup

If you've turned on Android backup, your device can copy your templates, settings and kept chats to your backup and restore them on a new device. That's a service you choose and control in your device's settings. Deaftly doesn't set it up or read it, and we can't see it. Kept chats can include anything you and the other person wrote, such as health details.

### Camera

The first time you read text with the camera, Deaftly asks for permission. It only uses the camera when you choose to read text with it. You can say no and type instead.

### Microphone

In a side by side chat, the other person can turn on voice typing to speak their replies. The first time, Deaftly asks for permission to use the microphone. It listens only while voice typing is on, it's the other person's turn and Deaftly is on screen. Speech is turned into text on your device. The sound is never stored or sent anywhere. You can say no, and you can both still type.

## This website

- **No cookies, no analytics and no tracking.** We don't use advertising or analytics tools, and we don't set cookies.
- **Visiting the site.** Like any website, our hosting provider handles your IP address and the pages you ask for, so it can deliver them and protect the site from attacks. It keeps this only briefly, for those purposes.
- **Fonts and images** are served from this site, so your visit doesn't send requests to anyone else. The feedback page is the one exception: it loads a check from our hosting provider that you're not a bot.

## Feedback

When you use the [feedback form](/feedback/), or **Report an issue** or **Tell us what you think** in the app, your message goes to the system One Lone Raven uses to receive feedback for all its products. The app sends it only when you tap Send. That's the only time the app connects to the internet.

<div class="table-scroll" role="region" aria-label="What the feedback form collects" tabindex="0">

| What | Why | How long |
|---|---|---|
| Your message, whether it's feedback or a problem, and the app version. The app adds its version for you; on the website, it's sent only if you give it | To understand and fix problems, and to improve Deaftly | 12 months after we've dealt with it, or after it was last updated if we haven't. Spam is deleted after 30 days. |
| Your email address, if you give it | To reply to you | As above, with your message |
| Your name and whether we can quote you, if you choose to | To show what you said on this site, credited the way you chose | Until you ask us to stop, or your message is deleted |
| A check that you're not a bot (website only) | To keep spam out | Not kept by us |
| The internet address your message comes from | To limit how many messages can be sent, which keeps spam out | Not kept with your message |

</div>

Please don't put health details or other personal details in your message.

## Who's responsible

Deaftly is made by One Lone Raven, which is responsible for your data under UK data protection law (the "controller"). If you have a question about your data, or want to use your rights, email support@deaftly.com.

## Your rights

The app keeps your data on your device, so you can delete it yourself at any time by deleting your templates or the app. This website only collects personal data if you send feedback. To see, correct or delete it, email support@deaftly.com.

## Changes

If we change what we collect, we'll update this page and say so in [What's new](/whats-new/).
