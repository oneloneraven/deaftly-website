# Privacy

This page explains what the Deaftly app keeps, what this website collects, why, and for how long.

## The app

Deaftly keeps what you put into it on your phone. We, the people who make Deaftly, don't receive any of it.

<div class="table-scroll" role="region" aria-label="What the app keeps" tabindex="0">

| What | Why | How long |
|---|---|---|
| Your templates, and what you type into them. This can include names, health details or routines. | So you can use them again | Until you delete them or the app |
| Your name, colour and other settings | To set up chats and Show the way you like | Until you change them or delete the app |
| Chat messages | To hold the conversation | Only while the chat is open. They're deleted when you end the chat. |
| Camera pictures and the text read from them | To add text to a message | Pictures are never stored. Text is kept only if you add it to a template. |

</div>

### Android backup

If you've turned on Android backup, your phone can copy your templates and settings to your backup and restore them on a new phone. That's a service you choose and control in your phone's settings. Deaftly doesn't set it up or read it, and we can't see it.

### Camera

Deaftly only uses the camera when you choose to read text with it, and asks first. You can say no and type instead.

## This website

- **No cookies, no analytics and no tracking.** We don't use advertising or analytics tools, and we don't set cookies.
- **Visiting the site.** Like any website, our hosting provider handles your IP address and the pages you ask for, so it can deliver them and protect the site from attacks. It keeps this only briefly, for those purposes.
- **Fonts and images** are served from this site, so your visit doesn't send requests to anyone else.

## The feedback form

When you use the [feedback form](/feedback/), we collect:

- whether it's feedback or a bug report
- your message
- your app version, if you give it

We don't ask for your name or email address, so we can't reply directly. Please don't include personal details in your message.

**Why:** to fix problems and improve Deaftly. **Where it goes:** a private list that only the people who make Deaftly can see. **How long:** until we've dealt with it, and no more than 12 months.

## Your rights

You can ask what we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it, through the [feedback form](/feedback/). Because the app keeps your data on your phone, you can delete it yourself at any time by deleting your templates or the app.

## Changes

If we change what we collect, we'll update this page and say so in [What's new](/whats-new/).
